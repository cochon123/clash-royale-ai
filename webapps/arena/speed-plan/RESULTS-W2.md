## What was built

- Derived bundle `deploy/arena/derived-bundle/` (gitignored, regenerated by the builder). It adds `scene.always_documents` and `scene.card_dependency_documents` and rewrites only `ui` and `effects` display trees and their atlas pages. Unchanged files are hardlinked from the source bundle.
- Content-addressed pack `deploy/arena/web-assets/` with `manifest-929656be6b7e.json` and short-cache `manifest.json`. JSON has gzip (level 9) and brotli (quality 11) siblings. PNG is stored as PNG. Bundle fingerprint `2a91c24521f50d679438755a9d17668f1bbf9cc12604e061bd245e2eb32a840e`.
- `deploy/arena/build_web_assets.py`, `deploy/arena/diff_bundle_display.py`, `engine/tools/web_assets.py`.
- `deploy/arena/package.py` includes the pack and no longer requires `clash-web-scene`. `deploy/arena/nginx-location.conf` serves `/clashroyale/assets/` with immutable caching, `gzip_static`, `application/wasm`, and CSP `script-src 'self' 'wasm-unsafe-eval'`.
- `clash-visual-check` accepts optional `--cards`. Empty `--cards` still calls `Painter::load`.

PLAN section 8 sketches a first visit at 25–40 MB and a 16-card worker at about 250–350 MB RGBA, with a gate of 450 MB. Compared with those figures:

- Wire bytes at or under 40,000,000: the self-pairs except pekka-bridge, plus hog-mirror × log-bait, hog-mirror × lava-loon, hog-mirror × golem, log-bait × lava-loon, log-bait × golem, lava-loon × golem, and golem × custom-mortar.
- Wire bytes over 40,000,000: every pair that includes pekka-bridge, plus hog-mirror × custom-mortar, log-bait × custom-mortar, and lava-loon × custom-mortar.
- Every preset pair's decoded RGBA is under 450,000,000 bytes. The largest preset pair is pekka-bridge × custom-mortar at 296,856,716 bytes.
- The worst custom pair is 496,347,612 decoded RGBA bytes and 105,989,744 wire bytes, above both the 450 MB worker gate and the 40 MB sketch. Of that RGBA, 87,892,828 bytes are the always-loaded pages (including the princess tower, the cropped effects page, and the king tower) and 408,454,784 bytes are the 16 cards' own atlases. The search is greedy marginal gzip wire, then pairwise swap to a local maximum. It is not a proven optimum. Decks: ['BattleRam', 'ElectroGiant', 'SuperArcher', 'RoyalGiant', 'SkeletonKing', 'DarkPrince', 'Wallbreakers', 'Ghost'] vs ['RamRider', 'Pekka', 'Knight', 'GoblinGang', 'SuperMiniPekka', 'HogRider', 'MinionHorde', 'SuperIceGolemite'].

## Visual proof

Rules, thresholds, scenarios, and fixtures were not edited. The checker binary gained `--cards` before the baseline, so both runs use that binary. The default load path does not pass `--cards`. Available RAM was about 5.4 GiB, and `visual_check.py` reserves 4 GiB, so the runs used `--jobs 1 --memory-gb 1`.

Baseline (full source bundle, quick suite) verdict FAIL with no baseline: 21 pre-existing error findings (`unresolved-art` for Baby Dragon, Tornado, Mega Minion, Rage Barbarian and related rows; `missing-logic` for Tombstone) and 2 flicker warnings. Accepted with a note that these are pre-existing presentation gaps, not a claim of official-client correctness. Report: `.visual-check/runs/20261024-112057-full-bundle-reference/` (gitignored).

Subset bundle, quick suite, `--assets deploy/arena/derived-bundle --expect none`: **PASS**. 0 new, 0 worsened, 0 fixed, 23 persisting findings. No changed rows. Report: `.visual-check/runs/20261024-112539-ui-effects-subset/`.

Direct diff `deploy/arena/diff_bundle_display.py --suite full --cards`: **0 failures** across 21 fixtures, 43,983 frames. For every fixture, draws and pose-texture hashes match between the full bundle and the derived bundle, and between the full bundle and the derived bundle loaded with that fixture's deck (`--cards`). Report: `deploy/arena/display-diff.json`.

## Contract deviations

- `effects_evolution` is in the coverage always-list and is not a document in this bundle, so it is omitted from `always_documents`. Listing it would make `Painter::load_for_cards` fail. Recorded in the report `deviations` field.
- Four prestige rocket export names are presentation seeds and are not exports in `effects.json`. They are not in the subset. The full document does not contain them either.
- `painter_wasm` is null. The manifest field is present. `--painter-wasm PATH` content-addresses a file to `files/<sha256[:20]>.wasm` when one is supplied.
- The size report has 21 preset pairs (every unordered pair, including self-pairs) rather than only the six self-pairs.
- The effects atlas was repacked as well as `ui_*.png`. Reachable effects regions did not fill the original page, and the raster check passed, so the smaller page is what shipped.
- `.br` siblings are written. `nginx-location.conf` enables `gzip_static` only. `brotli_static` is not turned on, because this change does not assume `ngx_brotli` is loaded.

## Open problems

- A 16-card pair of the largest atlases is still about 496 MB of decoded RGBA, over the 450 MB worker gate. The excess is character pages, not the HUD. Subsetting `ui` and `effects` does not bring that pair under the gate. PLAN section 8 already points low-memory devices at the stream view if the gate cannot be met.
- First-visit wire size is not uniformly inside 25–40 MB. P.E.K.K.A bridge pairs are about 50–60 MB.
- Lossless WebP would save about 32 MB across the whole pack (122.0 MB PNG to 90.1 MB WebP) and was not adopted.
- The quick-suite baseline still contains the unresolved-art and Tombstone findings. The subset neither fixed nor worsened them.
- `clash_painter.wasm` is not in the pack until W1's binary is passed to `--painter-wasm`.
