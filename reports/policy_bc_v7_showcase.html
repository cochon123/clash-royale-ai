Why this matters more than the win

A gain that survives the shuffled control is evidence the model learned something real about state. A gain that doesn't survive it is probably just extra capacity memorising the training set. v7 is the second kind — so scaling it would multiply parameters, not understanding.

The gates that stopped it

Pre-declared pass/fail rules. Two causal gates were decided and both failed, so the pilot stopped before the expensive evaluation gates.
