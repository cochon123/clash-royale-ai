08 · lessons

What v4.3 taught us

Capacity finally moved the needle after v4.2 proved the data recipe.

01 / CAPACITY

A 3.9× larger trunk helped.

Moving from 633k to 2.47M parameters on more data improved every primary offline action metric versus full-data v4.2, without blowing the 6GB laptop budget (peak ~749 MB).

02 / THINKING

Train random depth; toggle at serve time.

Sampling K during training keeps both the fast path and the deep path competent. At inference, --think-steps is just a compute dial.

03 / NEXT

Pair think with mirror TTA carefully.

Mirror TTA and think steps multiply latency. The next clean experiment is a frozen-manifest bake-off of v4.2★ / v4.3 off / v4.3★ before spending live phone time.

How to use it: default live to --think-steps 0 when latency matters; flip to 8 for offline eval or slower lab runs. Phone-lab accepts the same flag.
