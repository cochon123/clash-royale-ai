07 · interpretation

What v4.4.1 actually taught us

The diversity diagnosis is more important than a green readiness badge.

01 / FIXED

Card context now matches the action.

Training uses the human-selected card; inference and rollouts use the model-selected card. The placement head no longer receives a soft card mixture at evaluation.

02 / IMPROVED

Sampling recovers spatial variety.

19.5 unique tiles is materially broader than deterministic demos, and argmax spread reaches x=0.271, y=0.236.

03 / OPEN

Temporal mode reuse remains.

The policy’s favorite tile share is still about 2.1× human. A future objective should penalize repeated modes conditionally—not flatten every heatmap.

Judge warning: the realism scorer rates policy 0.941 versus real 0.875. Treat that inversion as evidence the judge is incomplete or exploitable, not evidence that the policy is “more human than human.”
