Lessons learned

  • v4.1 is architecture-identical to v4.0; trained on a larger RoyaleAPI cut (~28.1k usable battles vs v4.0's 22.5k).
  • More data helped: test slot top-1 +1.4pp, zone +1.4pp, XY MAE improved ~131 units vs v4.0.
  • Training crashed twice around epoch 17 under memory pressure; finalized from best checkpoint (no full 25-epoch completion).
  • Per-improvement checkpointing was added mid-project so crashes no longer wipe the run.
  • Judge on slot/zone/XY vs v4.0; live phone play remains expensive and is not required for this comparison.
