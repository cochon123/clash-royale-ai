02 · architecture

Belief first, then the action prior

Memory tokens are latent. None of them is advertised as a tracked living troop.

memory24 × 128 tokens. Friendly/enemy cross-attention, then two interaction blocks.
clockOwn and opponent rates. Survival of the quiet interval is a trained term.
actionType → selected card → 18×32 heatmap + within-tile offset.
p(next @ t, side s, action a | H) = survival(t|H) × rate(s,t|H) × p(a | belief(t), s)
survival(t) = exp(−∫ Σ rate(s,u) du) · piecewise-constant on 250 ms
