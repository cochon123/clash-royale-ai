01 · why classification

Mean collapse vs tile modes

If humans place left-bridge or right-bridge, regressing XY learns the midpoint. Softmax over tiles can keep both modes.

Continuous XY · mean collapse

Two human clusters → one predicted point in the river. That point is rarely legal or useful.

Heatmap · pick a cell

576 logits → sample or argmax a tile center. Multimodal placements stay multimodal.

grid: 18 × 32 = 576 tiles  ·  chance top-1: 1/576 ≈ 0.17%  ·  loss: tile CE (w=0.55) + zone + slot · xy_weight=0

08 · lessons

What the heatmap test showed

A small trunk can learn discrete placement modes when the loss asks for them.

01 / SIGNAL

Tile CE is learnable offline.

Top-1 at 15.5% and top-5 at 39.2% on a 576-way head means the model is not guessing randomly — the board structure is in the replays.

02 / TRADE

Card metrics stayed in family.

Slot top-1 55.34% sits next to v4.3 (55.58%) with a much smaller trunk. Zone 45.17% is the best of this trio.

03 / NEXT

Decode choice is the dial.

Train with CE; at serve time try argmax vs temperature sample. Pair with style matchup and matchup-lineage before growing K or trunk size.
